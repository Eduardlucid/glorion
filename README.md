GLORION Brand Divider
Purpose

The GLORION Brand Divider is the permanent visual separator used between the three equal vertical panels of every Services composition.

It is a core part of the GLORION visual identity and must remain consistent across all future illustrations.

Structure

Each composition is divided into three equal panels:

33.33% | 33.33% | 33.33%

Between the panels are two identical GLORION Brand Dividers.

The divider is not decorative.

It visually separates the story while maintaining a clean, premium appearance.

Both ends of the red segment taper smoothly into a sharp point.
The white line also tapers smoothly before every gap.
Geometry
Divider width: constant.
White line thickness: constant.
Red line thickness: exactly the same as the white line.
No gradients.
No glow.
No shadows.
No outlines.
No additional graphics.
Proportions

Using the divider height as 100%:

40% White

5% Gap

20% Red Core

5% Gap

40% White

The red core is centered vertically.

The divider is perfectly symmetrical.

Design Philosophy

The divider represents the GLORION engineering philosophy.

White symbolizes:

clean infrastructure;
precision;
reliability.

Red symbolizes:

the company identity;
the engineered core;
signal, energy and control.

The divider must always appear calm, minimal and premium.

It should never compete with the image itself.

Rules

Always:

three equal panels;
two identical dividers;
centered vertically;
same proportions;
same thickness;

## Services — Image Standard

### Desktop reference
- Service panel size: 201.67 × 520 px
- Approximate aspect ratio: 1 : 2.58

### Master image
- Recommended size: 1200 × 3120 px
- Format: WebP
- Quality: 85–90%

### Composition
- Main subject must stay inside the central 70–80%
- Outer 10–15% on each side may be cropped
- Important objects must not touch the edges

### Responsive behaviour
- One image set for all devices
- Images may scale and crop slightly
- No empty fields
- No distortion
- No stretching
